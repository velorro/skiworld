# Interview prep

## Deliverables (keep tight)

1. **Company snapshot** — product, customers, recent news (use web tools), why CS/RevOps matters there.
2. **Role thesis** — 3 bullets on how Rick creates leverage in 90 days.
3. **Story bank** — 4 STAR stories mapped to likely competencies (retention, expansion, forecast accuracy, cross-functional leadership, AI/ops tooling).
4. **Questions to ask** — 5 sharp questions (metrics, motion, team design, tooling, exec alignment).
5. **Risks / gaps** — honest prep on weak spots + how to frame them.
6. **Thank-you draft** — short, specific, approval-gated.

## Voice

Executive, concrete, metric-aware. Prefer “what I owned / moved” over buzzwords.
